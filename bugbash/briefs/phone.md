# Phone layout (390 x 844)

Start: `node bb.mjs start phone --phone`

Visit, in order:
1. `https://usesignals.app/` (home)
2. `https://usesignals.app/#/traders` (Leaderboard: open the period and market choices, scroll to the foot)
3. A trader page (tap a row from the Leaderboard)
4. `https://usesignals.app/breakdowns/?sample` (scroll the whole sample, and tap the market picker)

On each, look for:
- Anything wider than the screen (sideways scrolling), text cut off or overlapping, buttons too small to tap or
  squeezed beside other things.
- The Human/Bot pill beside the name should still read clearly.
- Menus and pickers that open off-screen or can't be closed.
- Sticky headers or bars covering content.

Take a `look` after every scroll. Never press Copy, Start, "Break it down", Ask or "Why here?", and never sign in.
